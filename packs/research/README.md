# Research Pack

**Unique close-proof:** a research output is "done" only when **claims are cited and cross-checked**, not
asserted — proven by a review covering `claims-cited` and `cross-checked`. Because source verification is weak
to machine-check, the structured `required_claims` + `evidence_ref` are what give it teeth.

`close_contract`: analytical `sources-verified` (standard). Usually declared.
