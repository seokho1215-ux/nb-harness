# source-verify — research pack skill

**Strengthens proof:** `sources-verified` — the research pack's close-proof this skill makes more accurate.
**When to read:** Before a research brief closes.
**Output / checkpoint:** a claim -> citation map and a second-source cross-check for the key claims.

## How
- Attach a citation to every material claim.
- Cross-check the load-bearing claims against a second, independent source.
- Flag any claim you could not verify rather than asserting it.

> Pack-scoped: this loads only when the **research** pack is active (declared ∪ observed ∪ implied). It does not
> ship in the flat core `skills/` set. See `scripts/pack-skills.mjs` for which active-pack skills to read.
